A LITTLE ABOUT THE WRITER

A little about
the writer ♡

Behind every page is a mind full of thoughts, stories waiting to be told, and feelings that find their way into words.

Meet Nikita Sharma, the writer behind Maple's World.

Her little corner of the world is inspired by her love for reading, storytelling, music and the many emotions hidden in everyday moments.

Her writing often wanders through deep thoughts, heartfelt shayari and emotions that aren't always easy to put into words. Sometimes it's a thought worth remembering; sometimes it's simply a feeling looking for a place to belong.

Beyond writing, Nikita enjoys getting lost in books, finding comfort in K-dramas, listening to music and watching the rain fall. Her cheerful, expressive nature brings a little liveliness to the world around her, while her love for the little details gives her writing its own charm.

For her, words are more than sentences on a page. They're little pieces of imagination, reflection and emotion.

And this is where those pieces come together.

Welcome to Maple's World — a place where every page has a feeling, and every feeling has a story. 🍁
